Common questions

I'm stuck on a word. How do I get a letter?

Tap the hint button above the keyboard. You can watch a short video to reveal a letter in the word you're on.

How do I check my answers?

Tap Check. Correct letters turn green, and you can keep going until the whole puzzle is solved.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Your progress and settings stay on the device, so deleting the app also deletes them.

How do I choose whether ads can fit my interests?

On iPhone, open your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Puzzle Club on or off. On other phones, open the app's Settings → Ads and privacy.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app saves stays on your phone, and deleting the app removes it. For information that advertising partners hold, see the privacy policy below, or email me and I'll help.
