PuzzleClub

Crossword puzzles with clues in English or Arabic.

Get it on Google Play

How it plays

  • Every answer is an English word. Read each clue in English, or switch it to Arabic with one tap.
  • Check your letters as you go: the correct ones turn green.
  • Stuck on a word? Watch a short ad to reveal a letter. New puzzles are added regularly.
